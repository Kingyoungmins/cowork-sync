## 1. Purpose

At a stand-up, people want to drop "what the office looks like now" into the team chat: a pixel picture of the selected room, or a few lines saying who last shared what. Today that needs a screen-capture tool. That tool also grabs the token-free but un-anonymized office, the map, the log and this PC's overlay. A **postcard** is one click in the viewer page. It makes either a 960 × 540 PNG of the current scene, drawn on the fixed recording canvas of 12 §3.9.4, or a short plain-text status in `L_h`. The user copies it to the clipboard or saves it as a file. Both are anonymized by default with the recording's own transform. Postcards add no server route, no served field, no board op and no network use. The page re-draws what it already holds.

## 2. User-visible behaviour

### 2.1 Control and panel

- A **postcard** button (`UI_VIZ_CTRL_POSTCARD`) sits in the bottom bar after `UI_VIZ_CTRL_RECORD` and is reachable in the same Tab order (12 §3.11). It works in live mode, in replay and in demo mode, for the selected room.
- Clicking it takes a **snapshot**: the selected room's scene at the position currently shown (the live tip or the replay position), the layout, the look table, the character and pet tables, and the anonymize state. The panel then opens with:
  - a preview of the picture: a `<canvas>` with `role="img"` and `aria-label` = `UI_VIZ_SCENE_SUMMARY` of the snapshot;
  - a read-only `<textarea>` with the text summary (§2.3);
  - the anonymize switch (§2.4);
  - the buttons `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_COPY_IMAGE` ("Copy picture"), `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_SAVE_IMAGE` ("Save picture"), `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_COPY_TEXT` ("Copy text") and `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_RETAKE` ("Take again"). The last one takes a new snapshot.
- **What you see is what you paste.** Every output is made from the snapshot shown in the panel, never from a later scene. New beats do not change an open panel.
- **`Esc`.** While an export panel (record or postcard) is open, `Esc` closes only that panel. The 12 §3.11 meaning of `Esc` (clear filters, and F32's "end a follow" if it merges) applies only while no export panel is open.
- Results go to an `aria-live="polite"` status line: `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_COPIED` ("Copied. Paste it into your chat."), or a failure code of §2.5.

### 2.2 The picture

- **Canvas.** The 480 × 270 art viewport of 12 §3.9.4 (office width 336 at x = 72, wall bands `#22222C`, a 224-pixel camera window, the caption strip at y 224–269), drawn at **2×**, so the PNG is 960 × 540 on every machine and DPR. It is drawn by the same viewport composer as the recording (§3.3), so the art pixels equal those of a recording frame of the same scene.
- **Camera (still).** The focus desk is the desk with the greatest `lastEvent` in `scene.desks`, ties broken by the lowest ordinal. The camera top is `max(0, min(focusY − 112, officeH − 224))` art pixels, where `focusY` is the art y of the centre of the focus desk's tiles (12 §3.5.2: a slot desk's two desk tiles; the big table's 4 × 2 tiles in the lobby, which always gives 0) and `officeH = 16 · (4 + 10 · ⌈cells / 3⌉)`. The outer `max` covers an office shorter than the 224-pixel window (no zone cell yet). With no focus desk, the top is 0 (the lobby).
- **Settled scene.** Every actor stands or sits at its `Place` (no walk in progress). Every state is drawn in its §3.7 **reduced-motion form**, at animation frame 0. States that last one beat or less are omitted: sparkles, paper planes, the event-sourced "!", puffs, confetti and icon-bubble pops. Every monitor screen is idle. **No bubble is drawn.** Persistent marks stay: hold-record "!", parcels, chair tags, badges, ghosts, ice, "zzz" and dashed future desks in replay.
- **Caption strip.** It shows the lines of the text summary that come before the first actor line, at most 3: the head, the demo or replay line (never both, §2.3 item 2), and the scene summary. These lines go through the same anonymize transform. The strip never shows card text or a demo caption.
- **Never drawn:** the local overlay (the "you" marker, the own room's lamp, count chips, `down`), the map, the log and the rewind bar (VIZ-13, VIZ-29).

### 2.3 The text

The summary is plain UTF-8 text with LF line breaks, rendered by the shared `render(code, params, 'viz', lang)` in the page's current language (`L_h` or the switcher's choice). Items inside a line are joined with the catalog separator `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_SEP` (en `", "`, ko `", "`, ja and zh-CN `"、"`, fr and es `", "`; drafts until I18N-08), so the client holds no separator literal (I18N-07) and the output does not depend on the browser's CLDR data. (`Intl.ListFormat` with `type: 'unit'` was rejected: it joins with no separator at all in zh-CN and with a bare space in ko and ja, and its output differs between Node's and each browser's ICU, which would break the byte-for-byte golden of T-F34-text.) The lines, in order:

1. The head. With anonymize off: `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_HEAD` "{room} · {branch} · #{height}". With anonymize on: `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_HEAD_ANON` "{room} · #{height}", where `{room}` is the rendered `UI_VIZ_ANON_ROOM`. The separator is `·` rather than `@` so that a branch named `here` or `everyone` cannot form a chat mention.
2. In demo mode only: `UI_VIZ_DEMO_BANNER`, at any position. For a real room in replay only: `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_REPLAY` "replay of an earlier point; live is at #{height}" (the room's `RoomVM.window.h1`). A demo is never live, so a demo never gets the replay line and the two lines never appear together.
3. `UI_VIZ_SCENE_SUMMARY` of the snapshot (the alt text).
4. One line per actor that has at least one item (below): the characters that are not ghosts, in `homeOrder`, each followed by its pets in variant order:
   - a character: `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_PERSON` "{name}: {items}";
   - a pet: `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_PET` "{name}'s Claude: {items}", or `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_PET_N` "{name}'s Claude {n}: {items}" when its owner has two or more pets in the snapshot (`n` = variant + 1).

   `{items}` lists, in this order, every item that applies:
   - the health label: for a character, `phOf(c, status, snapshot.at)` (8a `model/labels.ts`, R-V56) evaluated **at the snapshot's position**, where the page takes `status` from the scene character (`'absent'` when its `absent` flag is set, else `'active'`; a peer that left is no longer in the scene), not `CharacterVM.ph`, which is computed at `h1` and would be wrong in replay; shown when it is not `PH_OK` or null. For a pet, its scene `ssCode` when it is not `SS_FULL` or null (a healthy seat adds no item, as a healthy person does);
   - the place label from `placeLabel`: `UI_VIZ_HOLDING` "holding {label}", or `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_LAST_SHARED` "last shared {path}". The big table's path is null, so a publish there gives `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_LAST_SHARED_BIG` "last shared a large change-set";
   - when the actor's place is `facing` (an open request it filed): `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_WAITING` "waiting on {target} ({id})", where `{target}` is the holder's name and `{id}` the request's `R…` display id (glossary §3: `R` + 8 Crockford base32 characters).

   An actor with no item gets **no line**, whether character or pet. A postcard describes the room, not each person's output: a per-person "nothing shared" line would read as a productivity call-out once pasted into a team chat. The scene-summary line still counts everyone present. The rows are the rows of 8a's hidden people list (`peopleList`, `ui/a11y.ts`) in the same order and with the same place labels, except that health comes from `phOf` at the snapshot's position and actors with no item are left out.
5. **Limits.** At most 12 actor lines. Beyond that, one `UI_VIZ_MORE` "+{n}" line, where `n` counts the actors **with a line** that were left out (actors with no item are not counted anywhere). Each line is capped at 400 characters (the `viz` card cap, glossary §19 viewer row as proposed in 12 §9.2); when a line would exceed it, the item list is cut and ends with `UI_VIZ_MORE`. A summary therefore has at most 16 lines: the head, at most one demo-or-replay line, the scene summary, 12 actor lines and one "+n" line.

Example, en, anonymize off:

```
shop-app · main · #1234
3 people, 1 Claude sessions, 1 requests open, 0 conflicts
Mina: last shared src/cart.ts
Theo: last shared test/price.test.ts
Theo's Claude: holding applyDiscount, waiting on Mina (R7Q2K9MB)
Ana: paused (git outside cowork-sync)
```

The same snapshot in ko with anonymize on (the ko drafts await I18N-08 review; the health label is the 10-product §3.14 `PH_FROZEN` wording):

```
방 · #1234
사람 3명, Claude 세션 1개, 열린 요청 1개, 충돌 0개
살구: 마지막 공유 zone-1/file-1
하늘: 마지막 공유 zone-2/file-4
하늘의 Claude: fn-1 잡는 중, 살구 기다리는 중 (R7Q2K9MB)
초록: 일시중지(cowork-sync 밖의 git)
```

(If Ana had no health label and nothing at a desk, she would have no line; the summary line would still say "3 people". The "1 Claude sessions, 1 requests" wording is the 8a `en` catalog's `UI_VIZ_SCENE_SUMMARY`, which has no plural forms yet; see Review notes.)

### 2.4 Anonymize and the notice

- **One switch per page.** The record panel and the postcard panel show the same `UI_VIZ_CTRL_ANONYMIZE` control, bound to one in-memory page state. On every page load that state starts **on** for real rooms. It is never written to `localStorage`, `sessionStorage` or the fragment, so a reload or a new tab starts anonymized. In demo mode the control is hidden (demo data needs none, 12 §3.9.4).
- **Notice.** While anonymize is off for a real room, every open record or postcard panel shows `UI_VIZ_RECORD_NOTICE` above its buttons, and in that state the postcard's Copy and Save buttons are enabled only once the notice is in the DOM and visible. With anonymize on (or in demo mode) no notice is needed and the buttons are enabled at once. The notice's en template becomes: "With anonymize off, recordings and postcards show your teammates' names and file names; recordings also show their notes." The code is unchanged; all six catalogs are updated.
- **Same transform, one map per snapshot.** The image and the text use the same instance of the §3.9.4 transform, built once per snapshot. `file-3` in the text is therefore the `file-3` plaque in the picture. The rules: names become hue names; paths, zone signs and plaques become `zone-<i>/file-<j>`; labels become `fn-<j>`; the room and branch become `UI_VIZ_ANON_ROOM`; no handle is drawn. **New, also for recordings:** two or more characters that share both hue and accessory get `UI_VIZ_ANON_NAME_NTH` "{name} {n}", where `n` (from 1) is their rank in `homeOrder` among the characters of that look. Otherwise an anonymized text would show two identical names. Display ids (`R…`, `C<cid>`) are kept: they are board hashes, carry no name or path, and let a reader run `/cowork show`.

### 2.5 Copy and save

- **Copy picture.** Inside the click handler, synchronously, the page calls `navigator.clipboard.write([new ClipboardItem({ 'image/png': png })])`, where `png` is a Promise of the snapshot canvas's `toBlob('image/png')`. Passing a Promise keeps the call inside the user's activation, which Safari and Firefox require.
- **Copy text.** Inside the click handler, synchronously, the page calls `navigator.clipboard.writeText(text)` with the textarea's text.
- **Save picture.** The shared download helper (§3 item 2) saves the PNG as `cowork-postcard-<n>.png`. `<n>` is a per-page counter from 1. The name holds no date and no room name. The page adds no PNG metadata chunk (what the browser's own `toBlob` encoder writes is outside the page's control; T-F34-download pins the CI browser's output).
- **Unsupported.** "Copy picture" is disabled, with `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_COPY_UNSUPPORTED` ("This browser cannot copy pictures. Use Save picture."), when any of these is missing: `window.isSecureContext`, `navigator.clipboard.write`, `ClipboardItem`, or (where `ClipboardItem.supports` exists) `supports('image/png')`. Copy text has the textarea as its fallback, which the user can select by hand.
- **Refused.** A rejected write shows `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_COPY_FAILED` ("The browser did not allow copying. Use Save picture, or select the text below.") and is never retried automatically.
- **Never.** No clipboard read, no `paste` listener, no `document.execCommand`, no `navigator.permissions` query and no prompt. Nothing leaves the page except through the user's own clipboard write or download.

### 2.6 Codes (each with templates in all six human catalogs, channel `viz`)

New: `UI_VIZ_CTRL_POSTCARD`, `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_COPY_IMAGE`, `_SAVE_IMAGE`, `_COPY_TEXT`, `_RETAKE`, `_COPIED`, `_COPY_UNSUPPORTED`, `_COPY_FAILED`, `_HEAD`, `_HEAD_ANON`, `_REPLAY`, `_PERSON`, `_PET`, `_PET_N`, `_LAST_SHARED`, `_LAST_SHARED_BIG`, `_WAITING`, `_SEP` (each prefixed `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD`; `_SEP` is the item separator of §2.3, a symbol-only template with no placeholder), and `UI_VIZ_ANON_NAME_NTH`: 19 codes. The `{items}` placeholder is the viewer-rendered list of §2.3, declared as a `text` parameter (8a `render.ts` caps those at the 400-character card cap, without trimming whitespace, so the `_SEP` spaces survive); `postcardLines` itself cuts the list to 300 characters (ending with `UI_VIZ_MORE`) before rendering, so a line stays within 400. Reused: `UI_VIZ_SCENE_SUMMARY`, `UI_VIZ_HOLDING`, `UI_VIZ_MORE`, `UI_VIZ_DEMO_BANNER`, `UI_VIZ_ANON_ROOM`, `UI_VIZ_ANON_NAME_*`, `UI_VIZ_ACC_*`, `UI_VIZ_CTRL_ANONYMIZE`, `UI_VIZ_RECORD_NOTICE` (template revised), `PH_*`, `SS_*`.

## 3. How it uses existing mechanisms

1. **Data: the page's own view model.** The postcard reads only what the page already holds: `SceneVM` reduced by the page's `reduceScene` (12 §3.13), `CharacterVM`, `PetVM`, `LayoutVM`, the look table, `RoomVM.label`, `RoomVM.branch` and `RoomVM.window.h1`. All of it was served through the §3.10.6 allowlist and passed the validators, `sanitize(…, 'viz')` and, from Plan 8b, the D2 secret re-scan (VIZ-36). There is no new route, no new view-model field, no server code, no git call, no IPC, no `status.json` read and no board op. The board, folds, holds, seats and the visualizer's event model are unchanged; a postcard is a pure function of one reduced scene.
2. **Download.** The recording's Blob → object URL → `<a download>` path (12 §3.9.4 "Saving") becomes one helper, `src/viz/client/save.ts` `saveBlob(blob, name)`, which recording and postcards both call. It requires `name` to match `^cowork-(view-[1-9][0-9]{0,3}\.(webm|gif)|postcard-[1-9][0-9]{0,3}\.png)$` and revokes the object URL after the click. Every other export feature that merges adds its own fixed alternative to this one pattern (F29 proposes `cowork-reel-<n>.gif|.webm` "through the existing Blob download"; if F29 merges, that alternative is `reel-[1-9][0-9]{0,3}\.(webm|gif)` and F29 saves through `saveBlob` too). L-VIZ-DOM's single Blob-URL `href` exception moves into that file (§7).
3. **Drawing.** 8a's layer rule (plan rulings R-V33 and R-V35: `ui/**` imports nothing from `render/**`; `render/**` may import `model/**` and `ui/**`) splits the work in three:
   - the pure `src/viz/ui/postcard.ts` provides `postcardCamera(scene, layout)` (§2.2) and `postcardLines(snapshot, anon, render, lang)` (§2.3), and imports nothing from `render/**`;
   - the pure `src/viz/render/viewport.ts` provides `stillPhase(scene, layout): SpritePhase`, the phase of §2.2 (`reduced: true`, every `frame(actor)` the first frame of the actor's reduced form, `tile(actor)` = `placeTile` of its `Place`, every `screen(desk)` `'idle'`), next to the 8b viewport composer `composeViewport(static, sprites, cameraTop)` (bands, camera window and strip, shared with `record.ts`);
   - the browser-only `src/viz/client/postcard.ts` calls 8a's `composeStatic` and `composeSprites` (`render/raster.ts`) with `stillPhase`, then `composeViewport`, then draws text with `fillText` at device resolution, as everywhere (12 §3.7).
4. **Browser contract.** The viewer origin `http://127.0.0.1:<port>` is a potentially trustworthy origin (W3C Secure Contexts), so `navigator.clipboard` exists. The §3.10.4 `Permissions-Policy` denies `clipboard-read` and does not list `clipboard-write`, whose default allowlist `self` covers the top-level page. The page is never framed (`frame-ancestors 'none'`). The PNG preview is the canvas itself, because the CSP's `img-src 'self' data:` would block a `blob:` `<img>`. **No header changes**, so T-VIZ-headers is unchanged.

## 4. Privacy and safety rules

- **Never leaves this PC except by the user's click.** The page sends nothing anywhere. The only exits are the user's clipboard write and the user's download. The postcard is never posted to the board, a log, a file written by cowork or Claude's context. Once copied, the clipboard is the OS's: Windows clipboard history and cloud clipboard, or Apple's Universal Clipboard, may keep or sync a copy, and a web page cannot opt out of that. This is one more reason why anonymize starts on, and the notice (§2.4) covers it by saying what an un-anonymized postcard shows.
- **Never in a postcard (picture or text), whatever the anonymize state:**
  - teammate free text (`note.text`, `request.note`, `answer.note`, demo caption `text`, any later free-text field). There are no bubbles in the picture and no note item in the text. This is stricter than recordings, because a chat paste travels further than a video file;
  - `local_only` stub paths: a desk with a stub's lockbox shows the lock glyph (12 §3.5.3);
  - the local overlay (VIZ-13);
  - the map and the log;
  - tokens, the viewer URL, `127.0.0.1`, the room id, absolute paths, e-mails, the OS user or host name;
  - wall-clock times. Order and labels are heights only (I18N-03, VIZ-11).
- **With anonymize on, also never:** a served name, handle, path, label, branch or room label, in the picture (including the strip) or the text (12 VIZ-29 extended).
- **Chat hardening (text only).** In every parameter value inserted into the text, `@` becomes U+FF20 `＠`. A name such as `@everyone` or `@here` therefore cannot ping a channel. (A path such as `src/@types/x.d.ts` is shown as `src/＠types/x.d.ts`; that is the price of covering every parameter.) `sanitize(…, 'viz')` has already removed URLs, controls and bidi characters, turned `<` and `>` into U+FF1C and U+FF1E (no `<!here>`), and stripped leading `/` commands. **Not covered:** chat apps may still auto-link a file name that looks like a domain (`README.md`, `setup.py`, `notes.zip`) and may re-format markdown characters (`*`, `_`, `` ` ``, `~`) in paths and names; §9 items 4 and 5.
- **Clipboard rules.** The page writes only from a trusted click on a postcard button and only the snapshot shown. It never reads the clipboard (lint, §7).

## 5. Requirements

- **FEAT-F34-01** The page MUST offer the postcard control of §2.1. Every output MUST be made from the snapshot shown in the panel, and the panel MUST NOT change with later beats. _Covers: recording-leaks-team-data_
- **FEAT-F34-02** The picture MUST be the 480 × 270 art viewport of 12 §3.9.4 at 2× (960 × 540 PNG), drawn by the shared viewport composer. It MUST use the §2.2 still camera and the settled scene (reduced-motion forms, frame 0, no one-beat effect, no bubble). The caption strip MUST hold only the pre-actor lines of §2.3. The picture MUST NOT contain the overlay, the map or the log. For a fixed snapshot and anonymize state its art-resolution RGBA MUST be byte-identical on every run and OS. _Covers: recording-leaks-team-data, colour-only-meaning_
- **FEAT-F34-03** The text MUST follow §2.3: the codes and line order given there, `UI_VIZ_POSTCARD_SEP` between items, no line for an actor with no item, health from `phOf` at the snapshot's position, at most 12 actor lines plus one `UI_VIZ_MORE` line, at most 400 characters per line, LF breaks, and no human-text literal in the client. It MUST be built only from the page's view-model data. _Covers: phrasing-blame-and-peer-text-injection, path-quoting-and-git-locale_
- **FEAT-F34-04** Anonymize MUST be one in-memory page state shared with the record panel, on at every page load for real rooms and never persisted. Image and text MUST use one transform instance per snapshot. Identical looks MUST get `UI_VIZ_ANON_NAME_NTH`. While anonymize is off for a real room, `UI_VIZ_RECORD_NOTICE` MUST be visible before any postcard output can be made. While an export panel is open, `Esc` MUST close only that panel. _Covers: recording-leaks-team-data_
- **FEAT-F34-05** No postcard MAY contain an item of the §4 never-list. Text parameters MUST have `@` replaced by U+FF20. _Covers: recording-leaks-team-data, teammate-text-as-instructions, viewer-token-in-claude-context_
- **FEAT-F34-06** Clipboard writes MUST happen only synchronously inside a postcard button's click handler, using `ClipboardItem({'image/png': Promise<Blob>})` or `writeText`, with feature detection and the §2.5 failure codes. The client MUST NOT read the clipboard, listen for `paste`, call `execCommand` or query permissions. _Covers: xss-from-board-text_
- **FEAT-F34-07** Saving MUST go through the single `saveBlob` helper with the fixed name pattern of §3 item 2, and the page MUST NOT add any PNG chunk to what `toBlob` produced. _Covers: board-plaintext-and-permanence_
- **FEAT-F34-08** Postcards MUST add no route, view-model field, server code, git call, IPC, board op or network use. INV-VIZ-READONLY, INV-VIZ-LOOPBACK and the §3.10.4 headers MUST hold unchanged. _Covers: viewer-web-server-in-daemon_
- **FEAT-F34-09** (SHOULD) The panel SHOULD open, with its preview drawn, within 250 ms of the click on the Windows reference laptop for a 64-cell office.

## 9. Open questions (each with a proposed default)

1. **Notes with anonymize off?** The brief allowed teammate notes in a postcard once anonymize is off. *Default: never.* A paste into chat outlives a recording file, and notes are written to teammates' Claude sessions, not to a chat audience. Revisit if users ask, as an opt-in with its own notice.
2. **Picture plus text in one clipboard item** (`image/png` and `text/plain` together)? *Default: no.* Some chat apps then paste the text instead of the picture. Two buttons are clearer.
3. **Pull the anonymize transform forward into an 8a follow-up** so that postcards can be tested earlier? *Default: no.* 8a is never released, and every postcard export needs 8b's D2 re-scan anyway.
4. **Chat auto-linking of file names.** Slack, Teams and Discord turn a word such as `README.md`, `setup.py` or `notes.zip` into a link to that domain (`.md`, `.py` and `.zip` are real top-level domains), so a teammate-chosen file name can become a link to an outside site in the pasted text. *Default: no change in the postcard.* There is no plain-text escape that works in every chat app, and wrapping paths in backticks breaks on a path that contains a backtick. With anonymize on (the default) paths are `zone-<i>/file-<j>` and cannot form a domain. Revisit with a per-app "copy for Slack/Discord" format if users report it.
5. **Markdown characters** (`*`, `_`, `` ` ``, `~`) in paths and names will be re-formatted by Slack and Discord (for example `src/__init__.py`). *Default: no escaping;* the escape rules differ per app and would show stray backslashes in the others.
6. **Anonymize numbering across postcards.** The transform is built once per snapshot, so "Take again" could renumber `file-<j>` between two postcards of the same room unless the numbers follow stable keys. *Proposed default (for 8b's transform, which F34 reuses):* `<i>` = the zone index and `<j>` = the desk's rank among the room's file desks by ordinal, both append-only (12 §3.5.3), so the same desk keeps its number for the life of the page.
7. **Health labels in a chat paste.** A label such as `PH_PRESUMED_ABSENT` ("not answering") or a frozen person's reason is a board fact the viewer already shows, but in a chat channel it can read as calling someone out. *Default: keep them,* because they tell the team why work is stuck, and actors with no item already get no line (§2.3). Revisit if users find them unwelcome; the alternative is to show health labels only for pets.
