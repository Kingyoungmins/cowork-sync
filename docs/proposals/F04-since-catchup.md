# F04 — Catch-up summary (`/cowork since`)

**Status:** proposal (spec delta), 2026-10-08, against glossary rev 2.4 and the 12-visualizer draft of 2026-10-06; the newest glossary section wins. **Protocol change: none.** No op, board field, ref, push, fetch or network call is added; everything is computed on this PC from data it already has. Local additions only: one optional `meta.json` field (`since`, heights only) and two CLI↔daemon IPC frames (`since_query`, `since_ran`). **Depends on:** 00, 01 (ENG-05 meta), 02 §3 item 3 (Δmono and suspend), 04 (REG-34 `verifyLabel`), 05 (HLD-35 human CS), 06 (CCI-37 turn origin, CCI-46 budget, §3.16.1 `PULL_MODE`), 07 (§3.14–§3.17 digests, T-WAKE_RECAP), 10 (§3.3–§3.9, §3.12), 11 (TST-25), 12 (§3.3.5 timeline, §3.8–§3.9, §9.7 readers). F01, if it is ever merged, may add an `away` anchor; F04 does not define one. F29 depends on this file's `moments()`.

## 1. Purpose

After lunch, a meeting or a night away, a human wants to see in under a minute what the team changed and saved, who touched the files they were working on, and which requests wait for them; their Claude should start with the same context, so it does not "helpfully" revert a teammate's unfinished work (06 §3.13). F04 adds one read-only verb, one return recap at the first human prompt after an absence, and a catch-up panel in the viewer. The cursor is **board height** (glossary §3), never a clock (INV-CLOCK).

## 2. User-visible behaviour

### 2.1 Anchors (where "since" starts)

The range is `(from, to]` in first-parent board heights; `to` = height of the fetched board tip (as `/cowork log`, 10 §3.9). The daemon keeps three local heights in state meta (§3 item 1). **Human activity** on this peer = (a) a UserPromptSubmit with an empty `nonces[]` from any seat (06 §3.12.2; a doorbell or wake turn never counts); (b) a UserPromptExpansion of any `/cowork` verb, read-only verbs included, that 06 §3.12.2 does not classify as wake-originated (evidence minting is irrelevant here: 06 §3.4.1 mints evidence only for acting verbs, and a `/cowork since` expansion must count, §2.3); or (c) the opening of this peer's human CS (05 HLD-35), unless every path of its first delta is seat-tagged (HLD-35 bullet 3: a write after a TXN cap or from a background process a seat started), so a formatter or watcher left running by Claude does not keep the human "present" (§9 Q1).

| `--since=` | `from` | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| (omitted) | `meta.since.return_h` | the **folded height** `fh` (height of the board tip this daemon has folded) at the last human activity before the latest **return** (§2.3); falls back to `seen_h`, then, when this PC has no marks at all (fresh clone, state rebuilt, or before Plan 5 writes marks), to the height of this peer's newest folded `join` (the start of its current epoch) |
| `last-run` | `meta.since.run_h` | `to` of the previous accepted `/cowork since` on this PC (§3 item 1); `RF_SINCE_ANCHOR` if none |
| `save:<oid>` | height of the board commit carrying the effective `save` op whose `save_commit` starts with `<oid>` (7–64 hex) | ambiguous prefix → `AMBIGUOUS_ID`; none → `RF_SINCE_ANCHOR` |
| `#<n>` | `n` (decimal, no leading zero, ≤ 10 digits), `n < to` | else `RF_SINCE_ANCHOR` |

Any other value (including `away`, which only F01 could add) → `RF_SINCE_ANCHOR`.

### 2.2 `/cowork since [--since=…] [--json]`

Read-only (glossary §15), no human evidence needed, slash cap 4,000 and the 10 §3.3 frame. Sections in this order (truncation drops from the bottom, then `UI_TRUNCATED_SLASH`):

1. `UI_SINCE_HEAD` "Since #{from} ({anchor}) to #{to}: {n} board heights." plus `UI_COMMIT_DATE` of the `from` board commit (display only, as `log`).
2. `UI_SINCE_SEC_YOU` — items about **you** (this peer), first: (a) requests from or to this peer whose `request_status` or answer changed in the range: `R…`, direction, kind, other party, `{old} → {new}`, last verb and `by`, `UI_HAS_NOTE`; (b) this peer's hold records whose mirrored `hold` op in the range moved them to `PARKED` (`UI_SINCE_PARKED`; the `hold` op carries no cause, so the line does not say whether S6 or a human parked it, and HLD-61's `HOLD_AUTO_PARKED` notice already told the human), `offer=returned` (`UI_SINCE_RETURNED`) or `SUBSUMED` (`UI_SINCE_SUBSUMED`), and this peer's claims that became `STALE` (`UI_SINCE_STALE`); (c) teammates' CS groups that changed a path in **my paths** (`UI_SINCE_TOUCH` "{name} changed {path} ({labels}) in {k} change-sets"); (d) notes whose target is a request or claim of this peer, each inside the 07 `T-NOTE` frame; (e) this peer's own `origin=wake` CS groups, as a count pointing to the woken-turn recap (`UI_SINCE_WAKE`; never repeated item by item).
3. `UI_SINCE_SEC_SAVES` — ≤ 10 rows `UI_SINCE_SAVE` "{name} saved {c8}", newest first, from the effective `save` ops (`save_commit` as 8 hex). The save commit's summary is **not** shown: no v1 surface shows a teammate's typed save summary (`log` renders `UI_LOG_SAVED_AS` from the live chain, 12 §3.8 never reads save commits), and `git log` of the branch already shows it to the human without putting it into Claude's context.
4. `UI_SINCE_SEC_TEAM` — per teammate, CS group count, then ≤ 3 **areas** `{area} ({n}; {labels})` with ≤ 3 labels each. Teammates are listed in order of their first group's board position in the range, never sorted or ranked by count, and no total, rate or per-hour figure is shown (a catch-up list, not a scoreboard).
5. `UI_SINCE_SEC_EVENTS` — new hold records of any peer (count + ≤ 5 ids), `freeze`/`unfreeze`/`incident` events, joins, rejoins (new epoch), leaves, `presume_absent`.
6. `UI_SINCE_OLDER` "Older changes (before #{h}): /cowork log" when capped (§2.5); `UI_SINCE_NOTHING` when every section is empty.

Definitions. **CS group** = all `Cowork-CS` trailers with one `cs_id` in the range (its `checkpoint` commits and its `closed` commit count as one, 10 §3.9); a trailer counts only on a board commit whose live code commit changed (12 §3.3.5 step 2), so the CLI and the viewer count the same publishes (T-F04-agree). A group whose trailers carry no `Cowork-CS-Paths` (more than 256 paths, glossary §5) is counted with its `paths=<n>` value under the area `…` and never yields a touch, because F04 reads no live-code tree to recover its paths. **Area** = the segment rule of 12 §2 `zone(p)` (first two segments of a path with ≥ 3 segments, first segment for 2, `·` for root files; the viewer layout's overflow zone `…` is not applied), i.e. today's `zoneOf` in 8a's `src/viz/model/layout.ts`, moved to a shared pure module outside `src/viz/**` because `cli.js` and the daemon may not import `src/viz/model` (12 §3.13, L-VIZ-IMPORT). **Labels** = `label.simple` of folded `claim` records carrying that `cs_id` (Plan 4), and only labels that the daemon's `verifyLabel` (REG-34) has already verified, read over IPC (`since_query`); F04 never triggers a verification itself (that reads the claim's anchor blob), and with IPC down or a label unverified the label is omitted. Before Plan 4, paths only. **My paths** = paths of this peer's CS groups in `(from − 1,024, to]` ∪ paths of this peer's claims at `from` and `to` ∪ `paths` of this peer's non-terminal hold records (registry metadata only). `--json` prints the 10 §3.4 envelope, schema `cowork-since/1`: `data = {from, to, anchor, truncated_from|null, you: {requests, records, touches, notes, wake}, saves: [{peer, save8, h}], team, events}` (codes, ids, heights, paths, counts; no rendered text; note texts only as `you.notes[].text` after the §4 sanitizing). `truncated_from` is F04's range cap (§2.5); it coexists with 10 §3.4's `data.truncated` map, which still reports arrays cut to fit the slash cap.

### 2.3 Return recap (first human prompt after an absence)

- **Return** = a human activity when ≥ 30 min of `max(Δwall, Δmono)` passed since the previous human activity of this peer, or the first human activity after daemon start (the timer cannot survive a restart, ENG-05; "nothing changed → nothing printed" keeps that harmless). Both readings are this PC's own, taken through the single anchor helper (02 §3 item 3, PRO-10); this is INV-CLOCK's own-staleness form, as HLD-61 uses for hold age. Δmono alone would miss the main case: it does not count suspend (02 §3 item 3), so a laptop closed over lunch resumes with a small Δmono, the daemon survives (its lease timer is Δmono too) and no return would fire. A wall-clock jump can only cause one extra recap, never hide one. At a return: `return_h := seen_h`, then `seen_h := fh`, and one recap becomes pending. Every other human activity only sets `seen_h := fh`.
- The pending recap rides the **first human-origin UserPromptSubmit** of any seat of this peer (never a wake turn, never a doorbell), once per return; a newer return replaces an undelivered one. If the return was a `/cowork since` expansion, the recap is dropped (the human is already reading it).
- **Human:** `systemMessage` `UI_SINCE_RECAP` in `L_h`, ≤ 1,050 (glossary §19): head `UI_SINCE_RECAP_HEAD` "While you were away (since #{from}): {s} saves, {c} change-sets by {k} teammates.", ≤ 4 lines from section 2 (touches first; a (d) note enters the recap only as the `UI_HAS_NOTE` mark on its request or claim line, never as a `T-NOTE` block or note text), tail `UI_SINCE_RECAP_TAIL` "Full summary: /cowork since". It shares the one `systemMessage` with 10 §3.12 mirrors: mirrors first, the recap cut at line boundaries, tail always kept.
- **Claude:** one line `T-SINCE` (code `SINCE_POINTER`, `L_c`) in that prompt's digest delta, ≤ 300 chars, priority 7 (counts) of 07 §3.14; dropped, not shortened, when the delta has no room. en: `cowork-sync: since this user's last activity on this PC (#{from}): {s} saves and {c} shared change-sets by {k} teammates{touch}. The user can see the summary with /cowork since.`; `{touch}` (`T-SINCE.touch`, ≤ 2 paths): `; {path} (recently changed here) was changed by teammate {name}` (no "session": a human CS has none). ko: `cowork-sync: 이 PC에서 사용자가 마지막으로 활동한 뒤(#{from}) 저장 {s}회, 팀원 {k}명이 공유한 변경 {c}건{touch}. 사용자는 /cowork since 로 요약을 볼 수 있습니다.`; `T-SINCE.touch` ko: `; {path}(최근 이 PC에서 작업)를 팀원 {name} 쪽에서 변경`. The Claude line overlaps `T-ATTRIB` (06 §3.13 item 1) only in part: `T-ATTRIB` reaches Claude at the first PostToolUse after it touches a file of its read set; `T-SINCE` arrives before the turn's first tool call and also covers paths this peer claimed or published but this seat never read.
- **Nothing reportable** (every section empty) → no `systemMessage`, no line. `user.json` `since.recap: false` drops the human recap; `since.claudePointer: false` drops the Claude line (both default true). In `PULL_MODE` the Claude line is dropped (added to 06 §3.16.1's non-mandatory list) and the recap shrinks to its head and tail lines (≤ 2 lines), because the seat is already over its injected-chars budget.
- **Cost:** both are metered on the seat's injected-chars meter (CCI-46), the recap too, because a `systemMessage` can reach the model's context (10 §3.12). Worst case ≈ 1,350 chars per return against 20,000 per seat-hour; idle seats get nothing.
- With `T-WAKE_RECAP` in the same digest, `T-WAKE_RECAP` comes first and the pointer counts exclude this peer's own `origin=wake` items.

### 2.4 Viewer catch-up (follow-up after Plan 8a; product strings in 8b, §8)

- On page load each room shows a **catch-up panel** at the top of the log (page state, not a card): `UI_VIZ_CATCHUP` "Since #{h}: {m} for you, {t} team moments, {r} other changes" with **Play** and an anchor menu: **last visit** (default), **last save** (newest `save` event ≤ `open_h`), **since this view opened** (`open_h`), **#height**.
- **Last visit** = `lastVisit(t, you, open_h)`: the newest position ≤ the last position of `open_h` of a board fact made by **you** (the `peer` file's `peer_id`) or your pets: `publish` with `origin ∈ {prompt, human}`, `answer` with `by = human` whose holder is you or your pet, `request_open` whose `from` is your character (`origin = human`), or `save` by you. This is a board-only proxy: a long human-started Claude turn that keeps publishing `origin=prompt` after the human left moves it later than the real departure; the CLI's local marks (§2.1) are the precise anchor, and §9 Q2 keeps the viewer board-only on purpose. No such fact in the timeline the page holds → `UI_VIZ_CATCHUP_OLDER` with "load earlier" (pages of 256, ≤ 5,000 heights below `open_h`; beyond that the panel names `/cowork since`). `you` unknown (no `peer` file, demo) → no panel.
- `moments(t, from, to, you)` splits the events of `(from, to]` into **mine**, **team** and **routine** (§3a). **Play** walks mine ∪ team in position order: for each, `reduceScene` to the position before it and animate its beat at 1×; each run of routine events between two moments is one **montage beat** ≤ 1.5 s that sparkles every desk those publishes targeted (≤ 12, then `UI_VIZ_MORE`) with caption `UI_VIZ_MONTAGE` "{n} more changes by {k} people"; then the live pill.
- The rewind bar shows a page-drawn flag `UI_VIZ_LAST_VISIT` at the last-visit height (not a `Tick`, not served, never recorded). Teammates never get a visit flag.

### 2.5 Limits

Range cap 5,000 heights: above it the CLI and recap cover `(to − 5,000, to]` and say so (`UI_SINCE_OLDER`, `data.truncated_from`). CLI p95 ≤ 2 s at the cap on the Windows reference laptop; the hook reply never waits for the report (§3 item 3).

## 3. How it uses existing mechanisms

1. **State meta (01 §3.2):** `since: {seen_h, return_h, run_h}` — heights only, no timestamps (ENG-05); an optional, additive field (absent = no marks; no `meta.v` bump, LIF-37). Held in memory and persisted only inside state writes the engine makes anyway: the next transition, and the drain at exit ("finishes the cycle, writes state", 06 §3.17.1; LIF- stop), so the mark survives the usual daemon exit after the last session closes. When that drain write would otherwise change nothing, F04's dirty mark makes it one CAS update of `meta` only: the only state-ref update F04 can cause, at most one per daemon lifetime (01 §3.2 "Transitions only" gains this listed exception). A lost in-memory mark only makes the range longer (shows more, never less). The CLI gets `meta.since`, the verified labels and this peer's `MyRecord` list (registry metadata) over IPC (`since_query`). When IPC fails (and in the Plan 2 slice, which has no IPC) it reads `meta.since` from the `meta` blob of `refs/cowork-local/<e>/state` and the `records/<id>.json` blobs of `refs/cowork-local/<e>/holds` itself, metadata fields only (never a `blobs` oid's content), and shows no labels. `run_h` is set by the IPC frame `since_ran{to}` after the CLI printed, and the daemon applies it only from a `tty`-mode CLI or within 60 s of a human-classified `/cowork since` UserPromptExpansion (SEC-25's standard, without a refusal). Otherwise `run_h` is unchanged and the output is the same, so a Claude Bash call of `cli.js since` (slash-like environment, no expansion) cannot move the human's `last-run` mark.
2. **Board reading:** `readBoardCommits` (12 §9.7 item 8, batches ≤ 1,280: the commit object, `ops.jsonl`, `protocol.json`, the `held` tree object for entry names, `checkpoint/state.json` at multiples of 256), the fold from the checkpoint ≤ `from` (≤ 255 extra folds) with per-op verdicts (12 §9.7 item 1: only `effective` ops count), and the board commit's own `Cowork-CS` / `Cowork-CS-Paths` trailers (12 §9.7 item 2 `BoardCommitData.csPaths`), so no live-code tree or blob, no save commit and no branch commit is read.
3. **One pure core:** `sinceReport` = `sinceFinish(steps.reduce(sincePush, sinceInit(…)))` (codes + typed params, signatures below) in `src/since/report.ts`, a reducer so the daemon's incremental **accumulator** equals the batch form by construction. The daemon keeps one running accumulator fed by every commit its loop folds since `seen_h`. At a return the running accumulator becomes the pending recap's accumulator: its `from` (= `return_h`) is fixed and it keeps absorbing folded commits until delivery, so the recap covers `(return_h, fh at delivery]`; a fresh running accumulator starts at the new `seen_h`. A non-return activity just restarts the running accumulator at `fh`. After a daemon start the accumulator is rebuilt in the background from `seen_h`; if not ready, the recap waits for the next human-origin prompt.
4. **Hooks and carriers (06):** activity marks from UserPromptSubmit/UserPromptExpansion handling (nonce rule CCI-37), each setting `seen_h` to the folded height `fh`, the same height at which the accumulator is reset, so batch and incremental reports cover the same commits; delivery through the loader reply `system` and `text` fields (glossary §13); dedupe and `printed{batch_id}` confirmation as every digest (07 §3.14).
5. **Viewer (12):** `moments` and `lastVisit` live in `src/viz/model/moments.ts` (pure, compiled into the client, server and client run the same code); the only server addition is `RoomVM.open_h` (the `h1` of the room's first fold in this viewer process, which §3.8 already uses for "before this view opened"). No read is added to the 12 §3.3.2 read set; `Timeline`, golden files and recordings are unchanged (INV-VIZ-DET).
6. **Rendering:** one `render(code, params, channel, lang)` (10 §3.1); `UI_SINCE_*` and `UI_VIZ_CATCHUP*` in six languages; `T-SINCE` in en/ko (07). `T-SINCE` has **no** `MR_` mirror: 10 §3.12 derives mirrors mechanically from the 07 text and snapshot-tests them, and a hand-composed `MR_T_SINCE` would break that rule. The human counterpart is the independent `UI_SINCE_RECAP` (each can be on while the other is off, §2.3), so 07 §3.16.1 marks the `T-SINCE` row "no mirror" and 10 §3.12 / the I18N-10 check list it as their single named exception.

```ts
// src/since/report.ts — pure; shared by cli.js (since) and the daemon (recap). Codes and typed params only.
export interface SinceStep { data: BoardCommitData; verdicts: OpVerdict[]; after: FoldState; }   // 12 §9.7 items 1, 2, 8
export interface MyRecord { id: string; paths: string[]; state: string; offer: string; }   // registry metadata, never blobs
export function sinceInit(start: FoldState, from: number, you: PeerId, mine: readonly MyRecord[]): SinceAcc;
export function sincePush(a: SinceAcc, s: SinceStep): SinceAcc;           // returns a new value; never mutates
export function sinceFinish(a: SinceAcc, capFrom: number | null): SinceReport;   // sections of §2.2 as {code, params}[]
// src/viz/model/moments.ts — pure; server and client (12 §3.13). Position, SceneEvent, ActorRef, DeskRef, PeerRef: 12 §3.3.7.
export interface Moment { at: Position; lane: 'mine' | 'team'; event: SceneEvent; }
export interface Montage { from: Position; to: Position; n: number; actors: ActorRef[]; desks: DeskRef[]; }

export function moments(t: Timeline, from: number, to: number, you: PeerRef | null): Moments;

```

## 4. Privacy and safety

- **Nothing leaves the PC.** No op, trailer, board field, ref or network call (INV-F04-LOCAL, stated as FEAT-F04-16). Marks are local heights in the never-pushed state ref (01 §3.2), so collaborators-only and git-remote-only are untouched and nothing resembles telemetry.
- **Never read:** hold bytes (`blobs`, `held/` blobs), live-code trees or blobs, save commits and branch commits (their messages can carry real names, `Co-authored-by` e-mails and free text), the working tree, Claude transcripts or prompts (VD-7). Hold records are read for metadata (`paths`, state, transitions) only.
- **Teammate free text** is limited to notes (`note` ops and the `note?` of requests and answers). Notes appear only in the `/cowork since` output, only inside the 07 `T-NOTE` frame (as 10 §3.8 does for `/cowork show`), after `sanitize(text, "claude")` (SEC-34; slash output enters Claude's context) and a `scanSecrets` D2 re-scan (hit → `[redacted: secret-scan]`). Save summaries are never shown (§2.2 item 3). The recap, the Claude line and the viewer panel carry counts, display names, paths ≤ 80, verified labels and display ids only; the viewer never reads save commits (12 §3.8).
- **Not a scoreboard:** the recap and the Claude line carry only aggregate counts plus who changed *this peer's* paths; the CLI team section is ordered by board position and never ranked (§2.2 item 4); no surface shows another peer's activity times, rates or visit marks.
- **Clock:** ranges use heights and local marks only; the 30-min boundary is this PC's own `max(Δwall, Δmono)` (§2.3); commit dates appear only as the `UI_COMMIT_DATE` label of the CLI head, as in `log`. No remote "last seen" is ever computed or shown (UX-22, VIZ-12): anchors are computed only for this peer, and the viewer shows no time at all (12 §8 Q3).
- **No new trigger:** the recap wakes nobody, rings no doorbell, raises no OS notification, and a wake turn never moves a mark or consumes the recap.

## 5. Requirements

- **FEAT-F04-01** `since` MUST be a read-only verb of glossary §15 with exactly the flags `--since=<anchor>` and `--json`; exit 0 / 1 / 2 per 10 §3.4; it MUST change no ref, hold record, claim, config or working-tree file itself (INV-UX-READONLY).
- **FEAT-F04-02** Anchors MUST resolve exactly by the §2.1 table; an unresolvable value MUST be refused with `RF_SINCE_ANCHOR` (next: `/cowork since`), an ambiguous save prefix with `AMBIGUOUS_ID`.
- **FEAT-F04-03** The daemon MUST keep `meta.since = {seen_h, return_h, run_h}` as heights only (`seen_h` and `return_h` are folded heights `fh`), update it only on human activity (§2.1) and an accepted `since_ran` (§3 item 1), and persist it only inside the next transition or the drain's state write at exit (at most one `meta`-only CAS update per daemon lifetime).
- **FEAT-F04-04** A UserPromptSubmit carrying a cowork nonce, any hook of a wake-originated turn, any doorbell and a human-CS opening whose first delta is entirely seat-tagged (HLD-35) MUST NOT count as human activity; a non-wake `/cowork` UserPromptExpansion of any verb MUST count.
- **FEAT-F04-05** A return MUST be detected only by the §2.3 rule, with the gap measured as this PC's own `max(Δwall, Δmono)`; the recap MUST be delivered at most once per return, only on a human-origin UserPromptSubmit, and MUST be dropped when the return was a `/cowork since` expansion.
- **FEAT-F04-06** The report MUST list section 2 (you) before saves, team and events; MUST count one CS group per `cs_id`, counting trailers only on board commits whose live code commit changed; MUST use the `zone(p)` segment rule for areas and only daemon-verified labels (REG-34); and MUST order teammates by board position, never by count.
- **FEAT-F04-07** `sinceReport` and the accumulator MUST be pure and total (no I/O, clock or randomness) and the accumulator fed in any split MUST equal the batch report (canonical JSON byte-equal).
- **FEAT-F04-08** Reads MUST use the batched readers and checkpoints of §3 item 2, MUST NOT read live-code trees or blobs, save commits or branch commits, and MUST cap the range at 5,000 heights with `UI_SINCE_OLDER`; CLI p95 SHOULD be ≤ 2 s at the cap.
- **FEAT-F04-09** The recap MUST fit 1,050 chars after the mirrors that share its `systemMessage`, keep its tail line, shrink to head and tail in `PULL_MODE`, and MUST NOT be emitted when every section is empty or `since.recap = false`.
- **FEAT-F04-10** The Claude line MUST be one `T-SINCE` line ≤ 300 chars at priority 7, metered by CCI-46, and MUST be omitted in `PULL_MODE`, when `since.claudePointer = false`, when nothing is reportable, or when the delta has no room.
- **FEAT-F04-11** Teammate free text MUST be limited to notes and MUST appear only in CLI output inside the `T-NOTE` frame after SEC-34 and `scanSecrets`; save summaries MUST NOT be shown; recap, Claude line and viewer MUST carry no free text.
- **FEAT-F04-12** No F04 decision MAY use a wall-clock value from another machine; no surface MAY show a teammate's last-seen time or visit mark.
- **FEAT-F04-13** `moments` and `lastVisit` MUST be pure and total, classify exactly by §3a, and MUST NOT change `Timeline`, golden output, cards, ticks or recordings; the panel and the visit flag are page state.
- **FEAT-F04-14** Play MUST show every mine and team moment in position order and collapse each routine run to one montage beat ≤ 1.5 s that draws only desks of folded publishes.
- **FEAT-F04-15** Every new human code MUST render in six languages and `T-SINCE` in en and ko within the caps above (I18N-07..10).
- **FEAT-F04-16** F04 MUST NOT add an op type, op field, trailer, ref, push or network call (protocol unchanged).
