# coordination — Turn every situation in which two people's changes meet into exactly one recorded, explained and answerable conflict, without losing bytes (P1), without silent stalls (P2), and without burning teammates' Claude usage. Detection and enforcement stay in the daemon (publish gate B1, verifying writer B2, hold registry); this subsystem defines WHICH conflict kinds exist, WHO detects each one (exactly one daemon), WHO is told (causer once, holder once, bystanders only by pull), HOW a blocked change becomes a durable request and travels to the holder and back (U5), the closed negotiation protocol (request -> status -> answer -> done, patch-bound go-first, decline/release return, cycle breaking, starvation bounds without clocks), and every Claude-facing and human-facing text (fixed ko/en templates, size caps, voice per U6). The inbox socket (U7) is only a doorbell for idle seats; the record of every event is the board plus hook pull.

## v1 in scope
- Conflict taxonomy K1-K7 (claim-visible deny, claim-posthoc restore, claim-race hold, text conflict, incoming breach, human-in-claim, dependency/T0 hold) plus two non-conflict notices (CYCLE, FORK_SUSPECT), each with one detector, one record type, one template family (CN-01..CN-09).
- Canonical conflict identity (cid per (record, holder)) and request identity (rid = registry record), dedupe of repeats, causer/holder assignment from board order only (CN-02, CN-03).
- U5 flow end to end: deny -> SHADOW record born at deny time -> composition of chained denials -> OFFERED at the requester's CS close (any promoter) -> holder asked exactly once when its work finishes -> accept/decline/later/release -> return to requester; nothing deleted while non-terminal (CN-10..CN-13).
- Negotiation: patch-bound go-first with holder-side auto-checks and rate limits, decline ping-pong limits, release-with-reservation, liveness by T_ack/presume_absent, starvation visibility at 30 min, deterministic wait-for cycle breaking (CN-13..CN-16).
- Human-only override (/cowork override R<id>) that uses the gate's 'ask' outcome, never 'allow' (CN-17).
- Delivery: hook pull as the carrier of record with batch confirmation, per-seat cursors and successor seats; inbox doorbell only to IDLE seats for two event classes, under a wake matrix and budgets; turn-origin tracking and the wake-turn edit setting (CN-18..CN-21).
- Stop-hook truthfulness block plus holder asks, one block per turn (CN-22).
- Per-hook size caps, priority order, coalescing and the coordination share of the per-seat budget (CN-23).
- Templates ko/en: binding T1-T5 with variant clauses and render caps, and all additional templates (CN-24..CN-27); standing rules and attribution texts.
- Offline/suspend reconcile batching and the SUSPECT-claim deny text (CN-08, CN-15).
- Typed MCP tool surface for Claude (status, show, request, handoff, respond, note, release, checkpoint, resolve-restore) and the human CLI verbs used by this subsystem (CN-12, CN-17, CN-28).

## v1 refuse/defer
- v1: refuse — time-bound go-first leases ("you may edit for 10 min"). Go-first is patch-bound only: the grant covers exactly the recorded change (CN-14). Reason: LLM time estimates are uncalibrated and a board round trip outlasts a quick job (go-first-unbounded-and-slower-than-job).
- v1: refuse — automatic grants, dormancy and time-based claim expiry (binding protocol (11) and U5/U6). Starvation is handled by asking idle holders, presume_absent for unreachable machines, 30-min visibility and the human override (CN-15, CN-17).
- v1: refuse — per-hunk withdrawal, symbol splicing and any byte surgery to separate a causer's hunks (LCM-2/LCM-12). Race losers are held per change-set (CN-06).
- v1: refuse — free-form chat between Claudes. Only typed records (request/answer/note) exist; acks and thanks are not messages (CN-16).
- v1: refuse — an Edit/Write that touches functions held by two or more different foreign holders in one call is not routed to any holder; the record goes to the requester's human (CN-04).
- v1: refuse — doorbells (wakes) to seats in bypassPermissions, plan, auto or dontAsk mode, to unattended seats (no human prompt for 10 min) and to seats whose effective crossSessionInbound is hold/refuse or that run --bare; they are HOOK-ONLY (pull) unless the user opted in at /cowork start (CN-19). This is policy, not a host limit: own-child messages verified by token are delivered even in bypass mode.
- v1: refuse — a team-wide wake budget measured as a cross-machine time window (messaging-security MSG-DELIVERY 5). Replaced by a per-PC share derived from the fold's live-peer count, which needs no clock comparison (P5, CN-19).
- v2: support — human soft claims (humans taking claims from their saves). v1: humans take no claims; their edits inside foreign claims are held (K6, CN-08) and their stale-buffer reverts are caught by LCM-5 (human-claim-breach-and-claude-tramples-human, prevention half).
- v2: support — format-only hunk classification (token-stream-equal hunks in foreign symbols published or dropped separately). v1: format hunks are ordinary hunks; the CS is held and the T1 text says the overlap is format-only when non-whitespace characters are unchanged (CN-06) (formatter-whole-file-rewrites, classification half). Repo-wide formatter denies are claude-integration's advisory Bash rule.
- v2: support — interface-break detection through string literals and config keys (e.g. a JSON key read from C# and Python). v1: T0 catches arity, import-name, parse, duplicate-definition and shrink breaks only (c-interface-break-invisible-to-claims, config half).
- v2: support — Channels (notifications/claude/channel) as a delivery path; research preview, opt-in flag, not on Bedrock/Vertex/Foundry.
- Owned elsewhere (not re-specified here): f-save-lagging-tree-reverts-peers — save content is the engine SAVE resolution (tree of an existing live commit); this subsystem only renders the save report lines.
- Owned elsewhere: b-bypass-revert-of-unsaved-peer-work — LCM-5 revert detector and LCM-6 epoch freeze; revert-taint holds are never offered to any queue (LCM-9), so no negotiation applies.
- Owned elsewhere: write-staleness-check-precedes-hooks — claude-integration read lease; CN-27 standing rules carry the 'Edit, not Write' line.
- Owned elsewhere: provenance-rule-blind-to-withdrawal-deferral-save — the split states it targets are removed by LCM-12 (no withdrawal), LCM-16 (closure apply plus 'tests run without CS-<id>' Bash context) and LCM-17 (no splice); no NOTE-1 'incomplete' records are needed.

- CONSUMES (from engine): a LOCAL-ORIGIN entry point of the per-path verifying writer that writes a locally computed blob (CN-05 restore, CN-14 SHADOW merge) with CAS against the current disk clean blob (not against S_disk/M_disk of an apply), the same lock/EEXIST/round-trip handling, and deferral while a fence or Bash/PowerShell transaction on the path is open.
- CONSUMES (from local-change-mgmt): hold registry records (PIN, PATCH/conflict, SHADOW request records) with state machine ACTIVE/CONFLICTED/OFFERED/ACCEPTED/RETURNED/PARKED/SUBSUMED/GONE/RESOLVED_*; CS ids and CS close events.

## rules
- **CN-01** (P3, U6) The conflict taxonomy is closed: exactly seven conflict kinds exist, each with ONE detector daemon, ONE byte-preserving record, ONE recipient pair, and ONE template family. K1 claim_visible: the causer's own daemon answers a PreToolUse(Edit|Write|NotebookEdit) gate query whose predicted keys intersect a foreign HELD claim -> G1 deny + SHADOW record (CN-04). K2 claim_posthoc: the causer's own daemon finds, at PostToolUse or at Bash/PowerShell transaction close, a Claude-made change inside a foreign claim that was already folded when that call's PreToolUse was answered -> record + restore (CN-05). K3 claim_race: the later publisher's daemon, inside its publish gate B1 (including after every CAS re-merge), finds a CS hunk inside a foreign claim earlier in board order that was not folded at edit time -> whole-CS PIN (CN-06). K4 text: the later publisher's daemon gets merge-tree exit 1 on a path the insertion-concat fast path did not resolve -> data-handling conflict policy (CN-07). K5 breach: the holder's daemon, at apply, receives a non-holder change inside a symbol this seat claimed earlier in board order -> no rewind (CN-07). K6 human_in_claim: the human's own daemon, at publish, finds a human-CS hunk inside a foreign claim -> PIN (CN-08). K7 dependency: the later publisher's daemon gets a T0 failure on its merged outgoing tree (caller arity, local import name, parse, duplicate qualname, >50% shrink) -> CS held (CN-09). No other daemon may create a conflict for the same situation; bystander daemons only render the folded board (CN-18). Adding a kind requires a protocol version bump.  _(covers: single-canonical-event-and-delivery, warning-fanout-single-warner, idle-wake-amplification, a-race-window-overlap-leaks-code, disjoint-claims-textual-conflict)_
- **CN-02** (P2, P3) Detector inputs. (a) Only HELD claims (published, confirmed, folded) of other seats, plus CONFIRMED-but-unpublished claims of other seats on the same daemon (worded 'your other session (<label>)'), can produce a conflict; TENTATIVE claims never do, and a denied call takes no claim, so a call that never ran cannot create a phantom conflict. (b) Claim state is never an input to any hold-registry decision: a claim cannot exempt, release, narrow or re-key a hold (LCM-4). (c) A claim whose key cannot be resolved in the tree being checked (rename, move, parse error, grammar-version mismatch) covers its WHOLE PATH for that check; 'key missing' is never read as 'no conflict', and the template adds the unresolved-key clause (CN-24). Key overlap otherwise follows the region subsystem, falling back to stored-form byte-range overlap of the claim anchor. (d) Detection never depends on a hook having run: K3, K6 and K7 run in the publish gate for every outgoing CS whether or not PreToolUse/PostToolUse fired, so a failed-open or missing hook only delays the conflict to publish time; when K3 finds a claim that WAS folded at edit time (the gate should have denied), it is labelled 'visible', the seat is reported DEGRADED to claude-integration, and the CS is still held.  _(covers: fence-and-claim-leak-without-post-event, claim-launders-held-revert, rename-claim-bypass-generation-mismatch, gate-fails-open-on-every-hook-failure, mcp-gate-fails-open, fail-open-enforcement, version-skew-board-and-guards, race-loser-hunk-lands)_
- **CN-03** (P5, P3) Identity and blame. rid = the hold-registry record id (ULID), displayed as 'R' + first 6 base32 chars of sha256(record id) (extended by 2 chars on a fold collision). cid = 'C' + first 7 base32 chars of sha256(kind || 0x00 || record_id || 0x00 || holder_seat_id): one conflict per (record, holder). Every later deny, edit or state transition that lands in the same record reuses the same cid and rid; a text conflict on a path already covered by a record attaches to that record (LCM-7) and creates no new cid. Every claim conflict is announced on the board by the request/hold op that preserves its bytes (carrying cid and ckind); separate event{cid} ops exist only for kinds that preserve no bytes (breach, cycle, fork_suspect). Content follows code order and blame follows claim order (data-handling tie-break): the causer is the side whose claim on the overlap is later in board first-parent order, else the later code publisher. The label is 'visible' iff the holder's claim op was in the causer daemon's folded state when that tool call's PreToolUse was answered (the daemon records the fold tip oid per tool_use_id), otherwise 'race'. No timestamp from another machine is read; the word 'violation' never appears in any text.  _(covers: op-identity-cursors-board-merge, single-canonical-event-and-delivery, lease-expiry-clocks-heartbeats, phrasing-blame-and-peer-text-injection)_
- **CN-04** (P1, U5) K1 deny-and-record. On a K1 match the gate returns G1 (exit 2) with the T1 'visible' variant on the first deny for that cid in the seat's current turn, and T1R (one line) on later denies; from the 3rd deny of the same cid in one turn T1R carries the escalation clause. Repeats create no new event, request or notification. The SHADOW record is born at deny time (not at Stop), one per (causer seat, path, holder seat, CS): H0 = clean stored-form blob of the path at the first deny; H1 = the shadow file with every denied edit of that seat on that path composed in order, where each new denied edit is applied to merge-file(cur=current disk clean blob, base=H0, other=previous H1) so chained edits whose old_string contains an earlier denied new_string compose. If the rebase conflicts, the new denial opens a second record. H0/H1 are written with hash-object -w --path=<p> under -c attr.tree=<EPOCH_BASE> and -c filter.<d>.required=true for every discovered driver; raw tool_input is never stored; a filter failure keeps the record LOCAL-ONLY and pauses the path (BOARD-CONTENT 5). An Edit whose target spans functions held by two or more different foreign holders is denied the same way, but its record is addressed to the requester's human (/cowork queue), not to a holder (v1). The rest of the CS publishes unless LCM-13 bundles it (dependency on the denied text, breaking signature with a call site in the denied symbol, or T0 failure); if bundled, the T1 'held' scope clause is used instead of the binding 'remain shared' sentence.  _(covers: handoff-patch-rot-and-orphaned-returns, deny-workarounds-by-agent, board-plaintext-and-permanence, held-cs-strands-dependent-work, hunk-withdrawal-publishes-signature-without-callers)_
- **CN-05** (P1, P2, U5) K2 post-hoc restore is deny-equivalent and scoped. Trigger: the authoritative changed-symbol set of a Claude tool call (PostToolUse of Edit/Write/NotebookEdit, or Bash/PowerShell transaction close) contains a key covered by a foreign claim that was in the fold when that call's PreToolUse was answered (prediction miss from EOL/BOM/quote normalisation, replace_all, or a shell rewrite). Order: (1) record FIRST: a SHADOW record with H0 = the path's pre-image blob at call start (fence pre-image or the transaction's pre-stat snapshot) and H1 = the post-image blob, through the pinned clean path, plus a request op; (2) then restore the WHOLE effect of that call on each affected path (never per-hunk surgery): target = git merge-file -p --object-id (cur = current disk clean blob, base = post-image, other = pre-image), written by the verifying writer after the fence/transaction closes; (3) PostToolUse returns {decision:'block'} with the T1 'posthoc' variant. If the 3-way restore conflicts, or the path changed under a human-class write since, nothing is restored: the CS becomes an LCM-12 PIN and the T1 'held' clause is used. If the claim was NOT folded when PreToolUse was answered, K2 does not apply (the author's disk is never rewritten); it is K3 at publish. Human-class writes are never restored (K6). Paths of the same Bash call without a foreign-claimed hunk are untouched.  _(covers: bash-powershell-bypass-after-deny, deny-then-bash-workaround, edit-normalization-breaks-symbol-mapping, deny-workarounds-by-agent, posttooluse-restore-induces-workarounds)_
- **CN-06** (P1, P2, P3) K3 race loser. Inside B1 the later publisher holds the WHOLE CS as one PIN record (LCM-12): every path it touched publishes its LA entry and the author's disk keeps everything; later CSs that land in pinned paths or reference names defined only in the held CS join the same record. One request per distinct holder seat is created with the same record_id; if the CS was already closed when the hold was detected, the requests go straight to OFFERED (the causer's Stop has passed), otherwise they become OFFERED at its close. The CS publishes whole only when every intersecting claim is released, transferred to this seat, granted (go-first), or STALE. The causer seat receives ONE T1 'race'+'held' message for the whole CS, listing every (holder, symbol) pair up to the cap and 'and k more' beyond, through its next hook if mid-turn, or a CAUSER_HELD doorbell if idle (CN-19); the holder seats receive one T2 each by pull. When all intersecting hunks differ from base only in whitespace, the T1 adds the format-only clause. Offline reconcile: the first publish after an offline or suspend episode (CN-15) batches every K3 found into ONE OFFLINE_RECONCILE message instead of per-conflict T1s; the per-holder T2s are still sent by pull. Board ops for the hold and the requests ride the next push of any kind, so a code commit never lands without the claim state that explains it (board is the authority, protocol (9)).  _(covers: race-loser-hunk-lands, a-race-window-overlap-leaks-code, hunk-withdrawal-publishes-signature-without-callers, non-atomic-pair-and-fence, offline-split-brain, offline-auth-claims, formatter-whole-file-rewrites, e-formatter-and-write-livelock)_
- **CN-07** (P1, P3) K4 text conflicts and K5 breaches. K4: the data-handling conflict function decides content (TIP entry on disk and in the outgoing tree, LOCAL blob as a PATCH/conflict record); a successful insertion-concat produces no event at all. Recipients: the causer = the seats whose CS authored the local side (or the human, by status line) get T1X; the earlier author's seat (from the Cowork-CS trailer of the live commit that last changed those lines, read from the apply journal) gets T2X by pull only; nobody is woken for K4. K5: the holder's daemon never rewinds the tip. If the incoming breach merged cleanly, the holder seat gets T-BREACH(clean) by pull; if it conflicted, the holder's local version becomes the conflict record flagged claim-breach, publishing of that path pauses on the holder side, and T-BREACH(conflicted) names the record; /cowork resolve or cowork_resolve(id,'restore') republishes it as RESOLVED_PUBLISH, and the breaching change is queued as a handoff to the holder. An event{cid, kind:breach} op is published so that the breacher's daemon, on folding it, raises a doctor alert (gate or version bug) for its own seat.  _(covers: disjoint-claims-textual-conflict, human-claim-breach-and-claude-tramples-human, d-shared-insertion-point-collisions)_
- **CN-08** (P2, P5) K6 human edits and offline/suspend states. Humans hold no claims in v1. A human CS (unattributed writes, closed by 20 s quiet or /cowork done) with a hunk inside a foreign claim becomes a PIN record; no Claude receives a T1. The human is told with T-HUMAN_HELD as a systemMessage on the next hook of any local seat, in the status line, and by one OS notification per 30 min per PC; if not resolved within 10 min of local monotonic time, the record is OFFERED to each holder (T2/T3 name the requester as '<name> (human)'). Suspend/offline: while the daemon's own staleness (max(Δwall, Δmono) since the last successful fetch or push) exceeds 60 s or a suspend gap is detected, this seat's claims are SUSPECT; a PreToolUse on a SUSPECT key waits up to 5 s for a fresh fetch and, if the claim still cannot be confirmed, is denied with T-SUSPECT. Edits on unclaimed keys continue (G3) and their claims are tentative until reconnect, when CN-06's OFFLINE_RECONCILE batching applies.  _(covers: human-claim-breach-and-claude-tramples-human, sleep-offline-stale-and-suspect-claims, offline-auth-claims, offline-split-brain)_
- **CN-09** (P2) K7 dependency holds and the fork advisory. K7: when T0 fails on the merged outgoing tree (caller arity over the merged tree, Python 'from <local> import n', JS/TS relative named imports, parse, duplicate qualname, non-empty->empty or >50% shrink), the CS is held (LCM-12/14) and ONLY the author seat is told, by pull, with T-WITHHELD_T0 naming the check, path:line, symbol and the author of the other side; the other side is not notified unless its symbol is still claimed, in which case it gets one T4-style line by pull. No doorbell. Fork advisory: when a seat with an open SHADOW or PIN record on symbol S publishes a NEW symbol whose body has >= 5 lines and Jaccard similarity >= 0.80 over normalised 5-token shingles with S's current text, the publish proceeds and the author seat gets one T-FORK line by pull (event{kind:fork_suspect} for status). No hold, no holder notice, no wake. Held content on the author's own disk is announced to that seat at once (the hook after the hold is created), so its Claude neither builds on it as shared nor reports it as done.  _(covers: c-interface-break-invisible-to-claims, hunk-withdrawal-publishes-signature-without-callers, deny-workarounds-by-agent, held-bytes-mislead-local-claude, held-cs-strands-dependent-work)_
- **CN-10** (P1, U5) Request lifecycle. Every request is a hold-registry record plus board ops; it is created at deny/hold time and is durable once its registry commit is reachable from the remote board (LCM-8). States: pending_leftover -> OFFERED -> (holder daemon) request_status asked|queued_until_turn_end|awaiting_human|undeliverable -> answer accept|decline|later|release -> terminal via the registry (ACCEPTED->SUBSUMED, RETURNED, RESOLVED_*, GONE). OFFERED is reached at the requester CS close by ANY promoter: Stop, StopFailure, SubagentStop (for that agent), SessionEnd, the seat's next UserPromptSubmit, 10 min of local monotonic time with no hook from the seat, seat-lease loss, or cowork_handoff(rid); none is required. A request belongs to the requester PEER (clone) for that repo, not to a session: results and RETURNED items go to any live seat of that peer at its next hook, else to the next SessionStart digest of that peer, plus /cowork queue. A request is never deleted while non-terminal; board compaction drops only terminal records (protocol/LCM-8). Every request reaches a terminal state or stays visibly non-terminal: if the holder's daemon publishes no op within T_ack (CN-15), the request is RETURNED with reason holder_unreachable; a holder daemon whose protocol is below minReader shows up the same way.  _(covers: handoff-patch-rot-and-orphaned-returns, stop-handoff-silent-failure-and-reask-loop, version-skew-board-loss, local-only-holds-break-nothing-lost, zombie-claims-idle-holder)_
- **CN-11** (U5, U7) The holder is asked exactly once, when its work finishes. The holder's daemon classifies its seat locally: MID_TURN (hook traffic since the last UserPromptSubmit or wake, no Stop yet, or Stop with non-empty background_tasks), AWAITING_PERMISSION (Notification permission_prompt with no hook since), IDLE (Stop/StopFailure seen), ENDED. OFFERED handoffs: MID_TURN -> status queued_until_turn_end, asked in that seat's next Stop block (T3 compact, batched with all unasked requests); IDLE and attended and delivery live and within budget -> one HOLDER_ASK doorbell (CN-19) batching T3 for all unasked requests; IDLE otherwise -> status undeliverable, asked in the next UserPromptSubmit digest and the human gets one OS notification; AWAITING_PERMISSION -> status awaiting_human, OS notification, asked at that turn's Stop. asked_at is set when the carrying output is confirmed (CN-18); a request is never asked again. Afterwards it appears only as one reminder line '<rid> from <name> on <sym>, unanswered' in the UserPromptSubmit digest, at most once per request per 30 min of local monotonic time, at most 3 lines. A request ACCEPTED in a cowork-originated turn is re-carried once in full (T3 text plus 'accepted in a turn started by a teammate notice; not yet applied') in the seat's next human-originated UserPromptSubmit digest, so the holder sees the diff when its user is back. ## numbers
- Doorbell per seat: >= 10 min apart, <= 4 per 60 min (strictest of protocol 1/5 min, claude-integration and messaging-security 1/10 min; model: ~0.5M tokens per warm wake at the measured 511k median context).
- Doorbell per PC: <= max(2, floor(12/|R|)) per 60 min (team <= 12/h from messaging-security, made P5-safe by deriving each PC's share from the fold's live-peer count instead of a cross-machine time window; 4 peers -> 3/h per PC).
- Attended window: last human UserPromptSubmit <= 10 min ago (configurable 2-30; API-key users with a 5-min cache TTL may set 4 to avoid cold-cache wakes).
- Coalescing window: 10 s per seat; one message per (CS, holder) for K3; one doorbell carries everything pending for the seat.
- Caps: T1 <= 1,050 chars at worst case (binding en T1 is 672 chars of fixed text; with holder 20 x 4, sym 80, cid 8, rid 7 and the held-scope clause it measures 850, and 1,027 with the longest optional clause, so 800 cannot hold); other deny/withhold reasons <= 800; PostToolUse event <= 1,000 + attribution <= 600; UserPromptSubmit digest <= 1,500; Stop block <= 1,200; SessionStart <= 2,500 (rules <= 1,000); doorbell <= 1,500; all under the host's 2,000-char preview.
- Placeholder caps: names 20 rendered / 64 raw, sym 80, path 80, notes 280, compact re-send 200.
- T_ack = 120 s (protocol 10b) for the holder daemon's request_status; holder self-staleness 60 s by max(Δwall, Δmono) marks own claims SUSPECT; SUSPECT gate wait 5 s for a fresh fetch.
- Request age visibility: one OS notification per request per side at 30 min of local monotonic age; OS notifications <= 1 per 30 min per PC per class.
- Unanswered reminder: <= 1 line per request per 30 min of the holder's prompts, <= 3 lines per digest.
- Go-first: <= 1 open per (cid, requester); <= 1 per (requester seat, key) per 15 min; holder asked <= 3 go-first per 60 min (excess auto 'later'); grant bounded by the requester's CS landing (one governor window, p95 ~19-47 s per the governor model) or its next CS close.
- Decline: identical (requester peer, key, proposed_blob) not re-requestable from the same claim instance; 2 declines -> requester's human.
- Repeat deny: full T1 once per (seat, cid, turn); T1R afterwards; escalation clause from the 3rd deny.
- OFFERED promoters: Stop, StopFailure, SubagentStop, SessionEnd, next UserPromptSubmit, 10 min of local monotonic time without a hook from the seat, seat-lease loss, cowork_handoff.
- K6 human hold offered to the holder after 10 min (LCM-9 ladder).
- Fork advisory: new symbol >= 5 lines and Jaccard >= 0.80 over normalised 5-token shingles with a symbol the seat already has an open record on.
- cowork_status: one full result per 30 s per seat.
- Per-seat budget share (claude-integration): <= 20,000 injected chars and <= 4 doorbells per seat-hour; Stop blocks <= 1 per turn and <= 1 per request id ever (each Stop block ~1 full-context request, ~0.5M tokens at the measured median).
- Stop truthfulness skip: no block when every listed id already appears in last_assistant_message (saves one full-context request per turn with denies).
